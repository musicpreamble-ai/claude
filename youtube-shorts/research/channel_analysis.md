## Topic patterns (how THIS channel picks trending topics)

### 1. The polarizing "What If" hook = strongest format
- #8 "What If Elon Musk Took the AI Race to Space?" → **2.9M views (channel's best)**
- #7 "What If Earth Is a Supercomputer Running the Simulation?" → 63K
- Conclusion: **"What If + <huge name> + <trending arena> + <shocking outcome>?"** is the proven formula.

### 2. AI + tech-giant race is the channel's core obsession
- Facebook AI race, Google AI race, Elon AI race = 3 of the top 4 videos.
- AI is the channel's #1 trending vector.

### 3. Spicy CEO/celebrity drama works when it rides a live news wave
- Jensen Huang affair videos (2) were posted right as the NVIDIA news trended.
- Drama + money + fame = high CTR on the For You feed.

### 4. Science/philosophy "wow" questions — filler/evergreen content
- Death, simulation, immortality → lower views but evergreen, easy to produce.

### 5. Title style they use (every single one):
- Start with "Why", "How", or "What If" — a curiosity gap.
- Use a question mark.
- Name-drop a giant brand/celebrity when possible (NVIDIA, Facebook, Google, Elon Musk, Earth).
- Keep it under ~55 characters (fits Shorts title limit).
- Mix in hashtags only on some (#animation #science #scp / #space #science #solarsystem).
