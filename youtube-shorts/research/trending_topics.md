## How trending topics get picked up (working model)

Based on combining the channel's 8 videos + current news cycle:

1. **Watch the viral news spark.** A story must already be exploding across
   mainstream tech media (search interest, news volume, X/Reddit chatter).
   All 8 channel videos map to a story that was peaking when published.
2. **Find the "What If / Why / How" lense.** Never report the news plainly —
   always convert it into a speculative or controversial question
   ("What If Elon Musk Took the AI Race to Space?").
3. **Attach a giant brand or celebrity name.** NVIDIA, Facebook, Google,
   Elon Musk. Names = instant recognizability + curiosity in the first frame.
4. **Insert a shocking, counter-intuitive twist.** "Why you will never die",
   "Earth is a supercomputer" — a claim that seems wrong but makes you click.
5. **Ship within 24–72h of the peak** while search + feed interest is spiking.
6. **Match the Shorts feed's mood:** fast cuts, big captions, hook in < 2 s,
   length ~30–45 s, one clear idea, payoff/loop.
