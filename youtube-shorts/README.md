## How to use it (5 min)

1. Open **design/video_design.md** — it's everything you need, in order.
2. Pick a **title** (Section 3, option 1 recommended).
3. Record/Generate the **voiceover** with the script in Section 5.
4. Assemble scenes from the **storyboard** (Section 6) in CapCut (9:16, 1080×1920).
5. Add captions (ALL-CAPS style), post with Section 4 description, measure with Section 10.

## The concept in 3 lines

- **Hook (0–2s):** "Elon Musk just asked NVIDIA to bring Jensen Huang back. What if he takes over instead?"
- **Middle:** Why Musk needs NVIDIA chips (robots, Mars, xAI) → how he'd seize the board.
- **Ending twist (loopable):** "The real question isn't who runs NVIDIA. It's whether *anyone* should own AI. What do you think? 👇"

This is the same engine as the reference channel's biggest hit (**2.9M views**: "What If Elon Musk Took the AI Race to Space?") — a *current trending news hook* (Musk + execs petitioning to reinstate Jensen Huang) spun into a "What If" Short.
