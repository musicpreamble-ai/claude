## The 45-minute workflow

### 1. Voiceover (CapCut TTS)
- New project → **Create → Text → AI Voiceover** (or Text-to-Speech).
- Paste each narration line; pick a **deep male cinematic** voice. Settings: slightly slow, dramatic.
- One line per clip so each visual plays in sync.

Narration:
```
1. Elon Musk just asked NVIDIA to bring Jensen Huang back.
2. But what if the board says no? And Musk takes the whole company instead?
3. His robot empire runs on NVIDIA chips. So does Mars. So does his AI.
4. Three billion-dollar projects. One chip supplier. And he can't let anyone control it.
5. Watch this. First he buys ten percent of NVIDIA's stock. Then he pushes out Jensen's replacement.
8. The good news? AI gets faster. Cars. Robots. Research. The bad news? One person now controls the factory where all AI is born.
9. Not the Pentagon. Not a government. Just one man with the keys to every AI on Earth.
10. So the real question isn't who runs NVIDIA. It's whether anyone should.
11. What do you think?
```

### 2. AI visuals (CapCut AI Video Generator)
- **Create → AI Video** (text-to-video). Set **vertical 9:16**.
- Scenes 6 & 7 (pure beat scenes) can be generated too, or simply freeze/zoom a frame.

Scene prompts (copy-paste):
```
1. Cinematic dark server room, red emergency lights, giant NVIDIA logo glitching, low angle, tech thriller, 4K
2. Aerial tech HQ at night, dark corporate boardroom door slams shut, moody cyan lighting
3. Humanoid robot assembling a glowing chip in a dark factory, sparks, cinematic
4. Mars colony at dusk, electric cars, holographic AI interface, epic scale
5. Giant chess move in a boardroom, air of scheming, slow push-in, moody
6. Glowing "10%" holographic stock ticker above a skyline, cyan accents
7. Empty boardroom, glass table, one silhouette at the head of the table, ominous
8. Split screen: green futuristic city vs red dark server hall, one man silhouette
9. Silhouette on a throne of glowing circuit boards, world server map behind, epic
10. Zoom out: Earth wrapped in a web of light networks, one figure at the center
11. Freeze-frame, camera faces lens, question-mark energy, loopable
```

### 3. Edit + captions (CapCut)
- Timeline 9:16 1080×1920, import clips in order 1→11, voice on each.
- **Auto Captions** → style: ALL CAPS, bold, white + black outline; keep above bottom 480px.
- **Sound:** CapCut "Cinematic/Dark Tech" track; whoosh on cuts; heartbeat under scene 9; music dip during last line.
- Total ~45–55 s. Hard cuts.

### 4. First frame = thumbnail
- Opening card (0–0.6s): big text "MUSK SENDS NVIDIA A DEMAND", red/cyan split, bolt.

### 5. Export + upload
- Export 1080×1920 @ 30fps, H.264, no watermark (free tier exports clean).
- Upload as Short, title: `What If Elon Musk Takes Over NVIDIA?`
- Hashtags: `#Shorts #ElonMusk #NVIDIA #AI #TechNews`
- Post 6–8 PM local; judge by 48h stats in `video_design.md` §10.
